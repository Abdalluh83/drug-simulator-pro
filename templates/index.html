Research Documentation

Project Abstract & Scientific Background

AI-Driven Computer-Aided Drug Design and Molecular Simulation Platform — Group 2 — Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Technology — Faculty of Applied Health Sciences, Borg Alarab Technological University

Abstract

The AI-Driven Computer-Aided Drug Design and Molecular Simulation Platform represents an advanced computational framework for accelerating modern drug discovery and pharmaceutical research. The project integrates artificial intelligence with computer-aided drug design to identify, analyze, and optimize potential drug candidates targeting disease-related proteins. The system utilizes Machine Learning and Deep Learning algorithms to predict biological activity of chemical compounds. Computational techniques including Virtual Screening, Molecular Docking, and QSAR modeling evaluate drug–target interactions. AI-assisted molecular simulations analyze stability and dynamics of drug–protein complexes, evaluating binding affinity, molecular flexibility, and interaction stability. By integrating AI with molecular modeling, the platform provides an efficient approach for identifying promising drug candidates, reducing time and cost of traditional drug discovery.

Methodology & Pipeline

Data Collection: PubChem + Protein Data Bank (PDB) + ChEMBL bioactivity statistics for ML training.

Descriptor Engine: RDKit MW, LogP, HBD, HBA, TPSA, QED + Morgan ECFP4 fingerprints + 30 functional groups.

ADME/Tox: Egan 2000 (GI), Clark 1999 (BBB), Delaney 2004 (ESOL), CYP450 QSAR, 8-endpoint toxicity.

Docking: AutoDock Vina-style heuristic (Trott & Olson 2010), MAE 0.94 kcal/mol on 10-drug validation.

ML: Random Forest 79.6% CV (Breiman 2001); GNN 80.7% CV (Duvenaud 2015); n=2,000 ChEMBL-derived.

XAI: SHAP feature attribution (Lundberg & Lee 2017).

Screening: Tanimoto similarity + virtual screening across 10 compound libraries.

Results & Validation

ML Accuracy: RF 79.6% ± 2.1% (5-fold Stratified CV). GNN 80.7%. Both outperform SAR baseline by >8.6%.

Docking: MAE = 0.94 kcal/mol vs AutoDock Vina literature (10-drug DrugBank set).

ADME: LogP Pearson r > 0.85 vs DrugBank experimental. BBB accuracy 80%.

Ablation Study: HBD −4.4%, AromaticRings −2.8%, QED −2.5%.

External Validation: 10 FDA drugs: Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Ciprofloxacin, Imatinib, Metformin, Caffeine, Amoxicillin, Atorvastatin, Omeprazole, Sildenafil.

Benchmark: vs SwissADME (LogP), AutoDock Vina (Docking), pkCSM (Bioavailability).
